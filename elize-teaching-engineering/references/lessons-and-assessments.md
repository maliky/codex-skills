# Lessons and Assessments

Use this reference when consulting teaching archives, preparing `Qxx` sequences, producing teacher and student versions, or designing formative and summative assessments.

## Archive Evidence

- Treat `LaHerdrie`, `LIJM`, and similar archive links as read-only evidence. Resolve links before inspection and never rewrite the archived source through the teaching repository.
- Prefer the maintained editable source over its generated PDF when reconstructing structure. Use the PDF to verify the delivered result, not as authority for silently changing the source.
- Reuse structure, pacing patterns, exercise families, and assessment practice only after checking them against the current programme and current teaching assignment.
- Record whether an observation comes from an official programme, an archived colleague or user document, a current maintained file, or an agent proposal.

## Teaching Sequence

A typical evidence chain is:

`official programme -> contents and capacities -> progression -> Qxx sequence -> activities, course, exercises, corrections, homework, and assessment`

This is a routing pattern, not a mandatory lesson template. Preserve the repository's current sequence structure and the user's pedagogical choices.

- Make lesson objectives and capacity links checkable from the maintained source.
- Keep mental calculation or flash questions, activities, course content, exercises, corrections, and homework distinct when the local template distinguishes them.
- Do not invent a mathematical result, official expectation, classroom fact, date, score, student value, or assessment condition.
- When producing teacher and student variants from one Org source, control visibility semantically with the repository's supported tags, properties, or `:noexport:` structure. Do not maintain divergent copies unless the workflow explicitly requires them.

## Assessments

- Identify whether the task is diagnostic, formative, summative, homework, a common test, or another local category before adapting archived material.
- Check alignment between assessed items, taught capacities, expected techniques, allocated time, scoring, and any calculator or document rules.
- Preserve question wording when the user requests faithful reuse. Put proposed reformulations in a distinct draft or clearly identify them in the report.
- Verify both the candidate-facing surface and any teacher correction, rubric, or solution layer. Confirm that hidden material does not leak into the student export.

## `.DEV.org` and Review Comments

- Treat `.DEV.org` as a live task and decision surface when present. Update only the items resolved by the current work and preserve unresolved decisions.
- Treat comments whose marker is followed by `>` as active instructions. Implement them or report why they remain unresolved.
- Do not claim a synthesis or lesson is complete solely because files were inventoried; write the requested maintained result and validate it.
